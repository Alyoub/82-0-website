17-0 Game Support

Need help with 17-0, the free football draft game? Find quick answers below or contact us directly.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on this device. It does not sync between devices or browsers, and private or incognito windows do not keep it. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes it.

The game will not load

Refresh the page, check your connection and make sure JavaScript is turned on. Updating your browser or trying another one (Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox) fixes most problems.

Start over or delete history

Open Settings in the game. Abandon current run clears a draft or season in progress; Delete season history removes every saved season and resets your trophies.

Sound and vibration

Turn sound effects and vibration on or off in Settings. On iPhone, the silent switch also mutes game sounds.

Why did my great team lose?

Every game is simulated from your ratings, so upsets happen and ties are possible. Even the strongest roster rarely goes 17-0. Match your three coaching calls to your roster to tilt the odds.

How do I build a 17-0 team?

Start with a quarterback who passes and reads the field, add sure-handed receivers, a running back who catches passes and a defensive anchor with 90+ pass rush or coverage. The tips on the game page explain every rating.

Player names and ratings

Player and team names follow the same setting as the 17-0 iPhone app. Ratings are original game judgments, not official statistics. 17-0 is not affiliated with the NFL.

iPhone app: Game Center and multiplayer

In the 17-0 : Football Draft Game app, make sure Game Center is turned on in iOS Settings for leaderboards. For nearby multiplayer, turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both devices and allow Local Network access.

Still need help?

Email us and include the game name (17-0), your device and your browser. We usually reply within 24 hours.
