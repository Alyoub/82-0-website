Play the 17-0 game online

17-0 is a free football draft game you can play right here in your browser, on phone or computer, with no download and no account. It is the 17-0 NFL challenge: spin a random franchise and era, build a 17-0 team from five key positions, make three coaching calls and see if your team can win all 17 games of a regular season.

How to play

  1. Spin the TEAM and ERA reels to land a random franchise and decade, from the 1970s to the 2020s.
  2. Draft five players for your core: a quarterback, a running back, a wide receiver, a receiving flex (wide receiver or tight end) and a defensive anchor.
  3. Coach the season with three big calls after weeks 5, 9 and 13.
  4. Go 17-0 by winning every game. Ties can happen, and only 17-0-0 counts as perfect.

How to build a 17-0 team

  • Start with the quarterback. His passing and awareness drive the biggest share of your team rating.
  • Give him sure-handed receivers. Their receiving ratings feed straight into your passing attack, and QB–receiver timing adds chemistry.
  • Pick a running back who catches passes. An 82+ receiving back adds a chemistry boost on top of the run game.
  • Choose a real defensive anchor. He counts for 42% of your defense, and a 90+ pass rusher or cover man adds another boost.
  • Teammates from the same franchise or era add a little extra chemistry.
  • Match your coaching calls to your roster: air it out with an elite passer, unleash an elite defender, trust the chemistry when it is high.

Three ways to play

Classic shows every rating. Gridiron IQ hides ratings until your roster is locked, so you draft on football knowledge. Challenge removes every player rated 95 or higher.

FAQ

What is the 17-0 game?

17-0 is a football draft and season simulation game. You build a five-player core from random franchises and eras, then try to win all 17 games of a regular season for a perfect 17-0 record.

Is the 17-0 game free to play online?

Yes. The web version is free in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. Your drafts, seasons and trophies are saved on your device.

How do you build a 17-0 team?

Draft the best quarterback and receivers you can, add a running back who catches passes and a defensive anchor with 90+ pass rush or coverage, then match your three coaching calls to your roster. Even the best roster rarely goes 17-0, and that is the challenge.

Is there a 17-0 app?

Yes. 17-0 : Football Draft Game is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, with more game modes and Game Center leaderboards.

Is 17-0 affiliated with the NFL?

No. 17-0 is an independent fan game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or any team. Player ratings are original game judgments, not official statistics.
