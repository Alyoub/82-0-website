17-0 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you play 17-0, the free football draft game, on the web at 82-0.studio/17-0 and in the 17-0 : Football Draft Game app. 17-0 is made by Mouad Bousbaa (0 Studio).

Summary: you do not need an account to play 17-0. Your drafts, seasons and trophies stay on your own device. The web game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment details or precise location to play 17-0.

2. The 17-0 web game

  • Saved in your browser: your current draft, completed seasons, history, trophies and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent to us.
  • Hosting and fonts: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, these providers receive standard network information such as your IP address and browser type.
  • To show player and team names the same way as the 17-0 iPhone app, the game downloads a small settings file from GitHub Pages. The request contains no gameplay data or personal information.
  • Sharing: when you share a result, the text is copied to your clipboard or passed to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes.

3. The 17-0 : Football Draft Game app

On iPhone and iPad, the app stores your seasons, rosters, records and settings on your device. Optional features handle limited information:

  • Apple Game Center: your display name, player identifier, avatar and scores are used for leaderboards. Apple manages this account data.
  • Nearby multiplayer: your display name, draft picks and match results are sent directly between nearby devices using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. We do not run a server for these sessions.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google Mobile Ads and its mediation partners. Depending on your region and choices, they may process your IP address, general device information, advertising identifiers and ad interactions. On supported iOS versions the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; saying no does not stop you from playing.
  • Remote settings and connectivity checks: the app downloads a small settings file and checks for an internet connection. These requests do not include your roster, history or scores.

4. Your choices and deleting data

In the game, open Settings to abandon a run or delete your season history. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything the web game stored. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, and you can change ad and tracking choices in your device settings.

5. Children's privacy

17-0 is made for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@82-0.studio and mention 17-0 in your message. You can also visit 17-0 support.
