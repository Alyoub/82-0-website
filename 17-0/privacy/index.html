17-0 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you play 17-0 on the web at 82-0.studio/17-0. Section 4 is a separate notice about the 17-0 : Football Draft Game app. 17-0 is made by 82-0 Studio.

Summary: the browser game needs no account. Its code keeps game records in this browser and does not upload them. Visiting the page still sends network information to GitHub, Google Fonts and Google AdSense; AdSense may use cookies, local storage and other identifiers.

1. Information you provide

The browser game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment details or precise location. If you email support, we receive your email address and message so we can respond.

2. The 17-0 web game

  • Saved in your browser: your current draft, completed seasons, history, trophies and settings are stored in this browser's localStorage. The game code contains no upload of those game records to 82-0 Studio.
  • Hosting and fonts: GitHub Pages logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, and requests normally contain browser, URL and referrer details. The page also requests Inter and Outfit from Google Fonts, so Google receives normal network data such as your IP address and browser information.
  • Analytics and storage: the source contains no separate analytics library. Our JavaScript does not call the cookie API, but it does use localStorage. AdSense performs its own delivery, measurement and fraud-prevention processing.
  • Sharing: when you share a result, the text is copied to your clipboard or passed to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes.

3. Ads on this website (Google AdSense)

The page loads a Google AdSense tag immediately; the source does not pause the first ad request until a visitor makes a consent choice.

  • Before a consent choice: the request can send Google your IP address, browser/device details, requested page, referrer and existing Google identifiers. Region, browser signals, prior choices and AdSense account configuration can affect how Google handles it.
  • After allowing personalized ads: Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers and activity across sites to select ads.
  • Without personalized ads: Google says non-personalized ads can still use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. Limited ads still use IP addresses; programmatic limited ads can also use local storage and an invalid-traffic cookie.
  • Consent message: this repository contains no consent interface. Google can configure one outside the repository through AdSense, so this code audit cannot confirm the live message for every region.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Separate notice: 17-0 iPhone and iPad app

This section is separate from the website policy. The app source and distributable build are not in this website repository, so this audit could not independently verify the app's code. The current App Store listing advertises Game Center, nearby multiplayer and multiple game modes. Its developer-supplied privacy label says identifiers and usage data may be used to track, and that device ID and advertising data may be collected without being linked to identity for third-party advertising; Apple notes that this disclosure is not verified by Apple.

  • Review the App Tracking Transparency choice and other app permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Review Game Center data and the App Store privacy label for the installed version.
  • Do not assume the browser game's local-only save behavior applies to every app feature.

5. Your choices and deleting data

In the browser game, open Settings to abandon a run or delete history. Clearing this site's browser data removes its local saves. For the separate app, review its iOS permissions, Game Center and advertising settings; uninstalling removes its local container but does not necessarily delete information retained by an external provider. On the website, you can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. See the site's full privacy policy for provider links and further choices.

6. Children's privacy

17-0 is made for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. 82-0 Studio does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly through the website. Hosting, font and advertising providers still process requests as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
