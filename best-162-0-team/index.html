How good is a typical 162-0 draft?

We simulated 600 drafts where you always take the best player the reels show you. The median team wins about 143 games, and the top tenth reach about 148. That sounds great, but over 162 games it leaves the median team's chance of a perfect season at about 1 in 620 million. Even the best of the 600 drafts only reached about 1 in 61,000.

The gap comes from fit and chemistry: a true ace, a catcher who can field, power and contact through the order and a double-play pair. The 162-0 strategy guide covers every bonus.
