About 82-0 Studio

Last updated: November 18, 2026

82-0 Studio is the name behind 82-0.studio, an independent project that makes free browser games: sports draft simulations and the original 3D space exploration game Echofall. There is nothing to download, no account to create and nothing to buy on this website.

The games

  • 82-0 (basketball): spin a random team and era from the 1960s to the 2020s, draft a point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward and center, then add a sixth man. Make three coaching calls, after games 20, 41 and 61, and try to win all 82 games of the season.
  • 17-0 (football): draft a five-player core: a quarterback, a running back, a wide receiver, a receiving flex and a defensive anchor. Make three coaching calls, after weeks 5, 9 and 13, and chase a perfect 17-0 season. You can play in Classic, Gridiron IQ or Challenge mode.
  • 162-0 (baseball): draft and place a starting nine from random clubs and eras, make three calls during the season, and try to win all 162 games in a 30-club league. Every club and player in 162-0 is fictional.
  • Echofall (space exploration): pilot a 3D survey ship through the Veyra system, scan three original planets, explore their surfaces on foot and upgrade your scanner to recover a hidden resource cache.

How the games work

Every player has ratings. In 82-0, for example, they cover offense, defense, shooting, playmaking and rebounding. Your lineup's ratings combine into a single team strength, along with how well each player fits his position and how well the group works together (chemistry). Each game of the season is then simulated in your browser. The stronger your team is compared with that night's opponent, the better your chance to win, but no game is ever certain. In 82-0, no single game is better than a 97% chance to win, which is why a perfect season is so rare.

The ratings are our own judgments, set to balance the game. They are not official statistics, and a simulated season is a game, not a prediction.
