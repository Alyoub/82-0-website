A free browser space exploration game

Echofall is a short, original single-player 3D game. Pilot the Morrowfin through one star system, scan three distinct planets, choose a landing site and explore each low-poly surface on foot.

Collect lumen, return to your ship and install the Echo Scanner MK II. The upgrade reveals a hidden planetary cache, giving you a reason to launch, revisit a world and complete the expedition. No download or account is required, and progress is saved locally in this browser.
