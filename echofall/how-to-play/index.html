How to Play Echofall

Complete controls and a step-by-step guide to scanning Veyra's three planets, landing safely, collecting lumen, upgrading the Morrowfin and recovering the hidden Veilstone cache.

The objective

Chart Ruhn, Nacre-7 and Hollowmere. Choose any world for your first landing, collect six lumen, return to the Morrowfin to install the Echo Scanner MK II, then follow its signal to Nacre-7 and recover the cache.

Complete expedition walkthrough

  1. Fly toward the first marker. Use W to thrust, A and D to steer and Shift when you want extra speed. S brakes and can move the ship slowly backward.
  2. Scan each planet. Move close enough for the SCAN control to activate, press E and stay inside the scan range for 2.6 seconds. Repeat for all three worlds.
  3. Choose where to land. Once the system map is complete, approach any scanned world, slow below 4 km/s and press L. Each landing loads a separate surface scene.
  4. Collect three samples. Follow the surface marker and press E near a glowing sample. Each sample adds two lumen, so three samples pay the six-lumen upgrade cost.
  5. Refit the scanner. Return to the Morrowfin, press E to board and install the Echo Scanner MK II. If you upgraded away from Nacre-7, launch and follow the echo marker there.
  6. Recover the Veilstone cache. Follow the amber beacon on Nacre-7, collect the cache, return to the ship and seal the survey.

The three planets

Ruhn

An iron-oxide desert world with ferrite dunes, 0.38 g gravity and ferrite nodules.

Nacre-7

A tidal glass world with 0.71 g gravity, luma shards and the hidden Veilstone cache.

Hollowmere

A ringed ice world with nitrogen glaciers, low 0.27 g gravity and rime crystals.

Oxygen, heat and safe exploration

  • Surface oxygen: the suit carries five minutes of oxygen while outside the ship's air radius. Move within nine metres of the Morrowfin to refill it.
  • Automatic recall: reaching zero oxygen returns the surveyor safely to the ship with collected samples preserved.
  • Star heat: the hull temperature rises near Veyra. Turn away if the warning appears; the heat shield prevents the ship from entering the star.
  • Asteroid belt: collisions slow the ship, but they do not erase progress.

Echofall FAQ

Is Echofall free?

Yes. Echofall is free to play in a modern browser, with no download and no account.

How many planets are in Echofall?

The first expedition contains three original planets: Ruhn, Nacre-7 and Hollowmere. Each planet has its own small low-poly surface scene.

Why can’t I land?

You must scan all three worlds, move within landing range of a scanned planet and slow the Morrowfin below 4 km/s before pressing L.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are stored in localStorage in this browser on the current device. There is no account or cloud save. Read the Echofall privacy policy for details.

What happens when oxygen reaches zero?

The suit automatically recalls the surveyor to the Morrowfin and restores oxygen. Samples you already collected remain safe.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Echofall provides touch controls for flying, walking, boosting, scanning, collecting, landing and jumping. The device needs a modern browser with WebGL support.
