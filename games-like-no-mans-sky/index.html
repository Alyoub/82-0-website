Looking for Games Like No Man's Sky?

Echofall is a free original 3D space exploration game that runs in your browser. It offers a compact flight, scanning, landing, resource and upgrade loop for players who enjoy planetary discovery—but it is intentionally much smaller and simpler than No Man's Sky.

The short answer

If you are searching for games like No Man's Sky because you enjoy flying between planets, scanning unfamiliar worlds, landing and collecting resources, Echofall explores those ideas in a short browser game. It is not a replacement, clone or unofficial version of No Man's Sky.

Echofall focuses on one handcrafted star system, three distinct planets and one complete expedition that can be played without an installation or account.

Why space-exploration players may enjoy Echofall

Fly your own survey ship

Pilot the Morrowfin around Veyra, manage speed near planets and avoid the star's heat and asteroid belt.

Scan unknown planets

Approach Ruhn, Nacre-7 and Hollowmere, hold position during each scan and reveal their surface facts.

Land and explore on foot

Choose a cleared world, descend to its separate terrain scene and explore with gravity and oxygen systems.

Gather resources and upgrade

Collect lumen, return to the Morrowfin and install the Echo Scanner MK II to expose the hidden Veilstone cache.

What Echofall does not promise

A useful recommendation should make the differences clear. Echofall currently does not offer an infinite galaxy, procedural planets, multiplayer, bases, fleets, crafting trees, combat or virtual reality. It is a focused prototype built around one finishable survey mission.

That smaller scope may suit players who want a quick, free space expedition in a browser. Players looking for the full scale and systems of No Man's Sky should visit the official No Man's Sky website.

Play the complete Echofall loop

  • Scan all three planets from orbit.
  • Choose a planet and land below 4 km/s.
  • Collect three glowing samples for six lumen.
  • Return to the Morrowfin and install the scanner upgrade.
  • Follow the echo to Nacre-7 and recover the hidden cache.

Games Like No Man's Sky FAQ

Is Echofall a game like No Man's Sky?

Echofall shares broad ideas such as flying a ship, scanning planets, landing, exploring on foot, collecting resources and upgrading equipment. It is a short handcrafted browser expedition with three planets, not an infinite procedural survival game.

Is Echofall made by or affiliated with Hello Games?

No. Echofall is an original independent browser game from 82-0 Studio. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Hello Games.

Can I play Echofall free in a browser?

Yes. Echofall runs free in a modern WebGL-capable browser with no download and no account.

Does Echofall have multiplayer, base building or an infinite galaxy?

No. The current Echofall prototype is a focused single-player expedition in one handcrafted star system with three planets.
