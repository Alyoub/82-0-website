162-0 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you play 162-0, the free baseball draft game, on the web at 82-0.studio/162-0. 162-0 is made by Mouad Bousbaa (0 Studio).

Summary: you do not need an account to play 162-0. Your drafts, seasons and trophies stay on your own device. This website shows ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies (see section 3).

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment details or precise location to play 162-0.

2. The 162-0 web game

  • Saved in your browser: your current draft, completed seasons, history, trophies and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent to us.
  • Hosting and fonts: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, these providers receive standard network information such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Sharing: when you share a result, the text is copied to your clipboard or passed to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes.

3. Ads on this website (Google AdSense)

The 82-0.studio website, including the 162-0 web game, shows ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. The 162-0 iPhone and iPad app

The 162-0 app for iPhone and iPad is coming soon. When it is released it will store your seasons, rosters and settings on your device, may show ads through Google Mobile Ads (which can process device and advertising information, with App Tracking Transparency permission where required), and may use Apple's rating prompt. This page will be updated at launch.

5. Your choices and deleting data

In the game, open Settings to abandon a run or delete your season history. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything the web game stored. You can turn off personalized ads at any time in Google Ads Settings.

6. Children's privacy

162-0 is made for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@82-0.studio and mention 162-0 in your message. You can also visit 162-0 support.
