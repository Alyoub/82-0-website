162-0 Game Support

Need help with 162-0, the free baseball draft game? Find quick answers below or contact us directly.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on this device. It does not sync between devices or browsers, and private or incognito windows do not keep it. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes it.

The game will not load

Refresh the page, check your connection and make sure JavaScript is turned on. Updating your browser or trying another one (Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox) fixes most problems.

Start over or delete history

Open Settings in the game. Abandon current run clears a draft or season in progress; Delete season history removes every saved season and resets your trophies.

Sound and vibration

Turn sound effects and vibration on or off in Settings. On iPhone, the silent switch also mutes game sounds.

How does placing players work?

After you pick a player, choose his spot on the diamond. His natural position keeps 100% of his ratings; secondary and related spots cost 5% to 35%. Only pitchers can pitch and only catchers can catch.

Can anyone really go 162-0?

Almost never, which is the point. Strong teams win well over 100 games. To give yourself a chance, keep players at natural positions, chase chemistry boosts and make the right front-office calls.

Are the players real?

No. All 29 clubs and 1,044 players in 162-0 are original fictional creations. Eras are historical date ranges used to organize the game. 162-0 is not affiliated with MLB.

Is there a 162-0 app?

The iPhone and iPad app is coming soon. Until then, the full game is free here in your browser.

Still need help?

Email us and include the game name (162-0), your device and your browser. We usually reply within 24 hours.
