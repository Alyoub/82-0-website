Play the 162-0 game online

162-0 is a free baseball team builder you can play right here in your browser, with no download and no account, on iPhone, Android, PC, Mac or Chromebook. Like a fantasy baseball draft across eras, you spin a random club and era, draft and place your starting nine on the diamond, make three front-office calls and chase the impossible: a perfect, undefeated 162-0 season.

How to play

  1. Spin the CLUB and ERA reels to land one of 29 clubs and an era between 1950 and 2025.
  2. Draft and place any player from that clubhouse, then choose his spot on the diamond. Natural positions keep full ratings; pitcher and catcher are specialists.
  3. Make the calls at the end of May, at the trade deadline and in September.
  4. Play 162 games in a full 30-club league with live standings and extra innings. Only 162-0 is perfect.

How to build a 162-0 team

  • Your pitcher is your whole staff: pitching makes up 65% of your run prevention.
  • Keep players at their natural positions. Out-of-position players lose 5% to 35% of their ratings and cost chemistry.
  • Chase the chemistry boosts: an ace on the mound, a reliable catcher, middle-order power, contact hitters, outfield range and a double-play pair.
  • An average stamina of 80 or more avoids late-season fatigue.
  • Make the call that fits your nine: stack the power bats, play small ball, or buy the piece you are missing at the deadline.

A real pennant race

Every club plays 81 home and 81 away games. Watch the standings move after every game, track games back, shutouts, extra innings and walk-offs, and see where your nine finishes among 30 clubs.

FAQ

What is the 162-0 game?

162-0 is a baseball draft and season simulation game. You draft nine players from random clubs and eras, place them on the diamond and try to win all 162 games of a regular season.

Is 162-0 free to play online?

Yes. The web version is free in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. Your drafts, seasons and milestones are saved on your device.

Can you actually go 162-0?

Almost never. Strong teams win well over 100 games, but even the strongest possible nine goes 162-0 only about 1 in 450 seasons with the best calls. See the perfect season odds.

Are the players and teams real?

No. All 29 clubs and 1,044 players are original fictional creations, and eras are historical date ranges. 162-0 is not affiliated with MLB or any team.

Is there a 162-0 app?

The 162-0 iPhone and iPad app is coming soon. Until then, you can play the full game here in your browser.

Can I play 162-0 on Android or PC?

Yes. 162-0 runs free in any modern browser on Android phones, iPhone, iPad, Windows PCs, Macs and Chromebooks, with no download.

What is the best 162-0 team?

The strongest nine is built around a 95-rated ace and natural-position hitters with power and contact. See the best 162-0 team page for the full lineup.
