How good is a typical 17-0 draft?

There are 243 possible paths through a 17-0 draft: three pools in each of five rounds. If you always take the highest-rated player in the pool, the median team wins about 10.9 games and goes 17-0 in about 1 in 2,000 seasons. Even the luckiest tenth of those drafts only reach about 1 in 1,300.

The best roster above does several times better because it is built for fit: a passer with elite awareness, receivers he connects with, a back who catches and a defender with the right ratings. Picking for fit beats picking the biggest number.

A typical example: Brett Favre (94), Walter Payton (98), Jerry Rice (99), Randy Moss (98) and Lawrence Taylor (99). Its best calls (Air it out, Unleash your anchor, Go no-huddle) lift it from 1 in 2,000 to 1 in 920.
