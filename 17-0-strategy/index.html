Key takeaways

  • Even the strongest possible roster wins about 68.1% of its games, so 17-0 happens about 1 in 290 seasons at best.
  • Your quarterback matters most: his passing is half of your passing rating, and passing is 58% of your offense.
  • Chemistry can go negative: QB–receiver timing costs chemistry when your passer and receivers average under 80 awareness.
  • Unlike 82-0, playing it safe does not win here. The best calls lean into your strengths, starting with Air it out.

Why 17-0 is so hard

Every game is simulated drive by drive against an opponent rated between 74 and 95, with a small edge at home. Ties can happen, and only 17-0-0 counts as perfect. Small changes in your per-game win chance make a huge difference over 17 games:

How the 17-0 draft works

  • Five rounds: quarterback, running back, wide receiver, receiving flex (wide receiver or tight end) and defensive anchor (EDGE, DL, LB, CB or S).
  • One spin per round: the TEAM and ERA reels land on a franchise and decade. Only the deepest pools come up, three per round. See them all on the best 17-0 team cheat sheet.
  • Pick one player from up to 25 in that pool, sorted by rating. A player can only be drafted once.
  • One respin per draft, if a pool is weak.
  • Three modes: Classic shows every rating, Gridiron IQ hides them, and Challenge removes every player rated 95 or higher.

What this means: the quarterback is the single biggest lever. Your defensive anchor only moves 42% of your defense, so a great passer and receivers usually matter more than a star defender. Overall ratings are not used directly; the specific ratings above are.

Round-by-round picks

  1. Quarterback: passing first, then awareness. High awareness also powers timing chemistry.
  2. Running back: rushing first. A back with 82+ receiving adds +2 chemistry.
  3. Wide receiver: receiving and awareness. A receiver from your quarterback's team or era adds chemistry.
  4. Receiving flex: same rules. A tight end can win this round when his receiving and awareness are higher.
  5. Defensive anchor: look at the defense rating, not the overall, and grab 90+ pass rush or coverage for +2 chemistry.
