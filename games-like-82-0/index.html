What makes a game like 82-0

  • Random spins: TEAM and ERA reels decide which players you can pick, so every draft is different.
  • All-time legends: players from every decade side by side, with ratings for every part of their game.
  • Fit and chemistry: positions, roles and connections matter as much as overall ratings.
  • Coaching calls: three decisions during the season can make or break a perfect run.
  • A perfect record to chase: the goal is never just a good season. It is an unbeaten one.

Play on any device

All three games run in any modern browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox on Android phones, iPhone, iPad, Windows PCs, Macs and Chromebooks. There is nothing to download and no account to create, and your seasons and trophies are saved on your device.

On iPhone and iPad you can also get 82-0: Basketball Draft and 17-0 : Football Draft Game from the App Store. The 162-0 app is coming soon.
