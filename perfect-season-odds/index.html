Why perfect seasons are so rare

A perfect season means winning every single game. If your team wins a share p of its games, the chance of winning all n is pn. That number collapses fast as seasons get longer:

Win chance per game vs. chance of a perfect season
Win chance per game17 games82 games162 games
90%1 in 61 in 5,7001 in 26 million
95%1 in 21 in 671 in 4,100
98%1 in 11 in 51 in 26
99%1 in 11 in 21 in 5

That is why 162-0 is so much harder than 17-0, even though the best baseball team in our study wins a bigger share of its games than the best football team.

17-0: the best and typical rosters

The strongest roster you can draft in 17-0 (Joe Montana, Walter Payton, Jerry Rice, Reggie Wayne and Jerome Brown) wins 68.1% of its games and goes 17-0 in about 1 in 290 seasons with the best coaching calls. Without any calls it is 1 in 690.

We also played out all 243 possible drafts where you always take the highest-rated player. The median team wins 10.9 games and goes 17-0 in about 1 in 2,000 seasons. In football, building for fit and chemistry matters more than raw ratings.

162-0: why baseball is different

The strongest nine in 162-0 wins 95.6% of its games, about 156 wins a season with the best calls, and still goes 162-0 only about 1 in 450 seasons.

A typical good draft wins about 143 games, far more than any real team, but its chance of a perfect season is about 1 in 620 million. Across 600 simulated drafts, the best one reached only 1 in 61,000.

Real-life records

  • Basketball: no NBA team has gone 82-0. The best regular season is 73-9, by the 2015–16 Golden State Warriors, ahead of the 1995–96 Chicago Bulls at 72-10.
  • Football: the 1972 Miami Dolphins finished 17-0 including the playoffs (14-0 in the regular season). The 2007 New England Patriots went 16-0 in the regular season but lost the Super Bowl.
  • Baseball: the most wins in a 162-game season is 116, by the 2001 Seattle Mariners (116-46).

How we calculated this

  • Same engines as the games. Every number comes from the code that runs 82-0, 17-0 and 162-0 on the web.
  • Exact per-game odds, not random samples. 82-0 integrates the win chance over the opponent distribution; 17-0 convolves drive-by-drive score distributions; 162-0 uses the Poisson run model with extra innings.
  • Season odds multiply the chance of winning every game, with home and away games and late-season fatigue where they apply.
  • Best coaching path: all 27 combinations of the three calls were tested for each roster.
  • Best rosters only use players the reels can actually offer. Typical drafts always take the best player shown: 2,000 drafts for 82-0, all 243 possible drafts for 17-0 and 600 drafts for 162-0.
  • Data as of November 9, 2026. Ratings are original game ratings, not official statistics.
