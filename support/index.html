Help & contact

Answers to common questions about the free 82-0 basketball game on this website. For the other games, see 17-0 help and 162-0 help.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser, on this device. Your draft, seasons, trophies and settings are kept in the browser's local storage, so they do not move to another browser or device. Private or incognito windows usually clear them when you close the window.

How do I start over?

Open Settings. Abandon current run clears the draft or season in progress and keeps your history. Delete season history removes every saved season and resets your trophies on this device.

The game will not load

If you see "The player database could not be loaded", check your internet connection and refresh the page. The game needs JavaScript and an up-to-date version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.

How does a draft work?

For each position you spin for a random team and era, then draft one player from that pool. After five starters you add a sixth man, and during the 82-game season you make three coaching calls. You get one Respin per draft. The strategy guide explains the scoring.

Sound and vibration

Turn sound effects on or off in Settings. The vibration switch appears only on phones that support it.
