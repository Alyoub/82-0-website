Play the 82-0 game online

82-0 is a free basketball draft challenge you can play right here in your browser, on desktop or phone, with no download and no account. Your goal is the hardest record in basketball: a perfect 82 and 0 regular season.

How to play 82-0

  1. Spin the TEAM and ERA reels to land a random franchise and decade from the 1960s to the 2020s.
  2. Draft one player from that pool for each position: point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward and center.
  3. Add a sixth man of any position to boost your bench.
  4. Coach the season: make three coaching calls after games 20, 41 and 61 and watch all 82 games play out.

Want an edge? Read the 82-0 strategy guide, learn how 82-0 works, or take your dynasty on the go with the 82-0 app for iPhone and iPad.
