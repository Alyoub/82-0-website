Tips for drafting the Warriors

  • Shooting guard is the deepest Warriors spot, with reports from 4 different eras.
  • The 2010s Warriors cover the most positions (shooting guard and small forward). Landing them early keeps your options open.
  • Players at a secondary position count at 95% and cost 1 point of chemistry. A slightly lower-rated natural fit can be the better pick.
  • Only one Respin per draft. Save it for point guard or center, where pools are thinnest.
