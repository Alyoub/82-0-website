Key takeaways

  • The Heat have 8 starting-position pools and 3 sixth-man pools, all from the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s. No 1990s Heat pool has enough players to appear.
  • LeBron James is a 2010s small forward, power forward or sixth man for Miami, never a point guard: the 2010s Heat point guard pool has only three players.
  • In the 2000s point guard pool, Dwyane Wade and Gary Payton are dead even for team power. Choose by need: Payton's defense or Wade's scoring.
  • Shaquille O'Neal and Alonzo Mourning never appear in a Heat center pool. The only Heat center pool is the 2010s one.
  • Built only from Heat pools, the best five averages about 76 wins and goes 82-0 about 1 in 555 seasons with the best coaching calls.

Heat pool counts and how reports are chosen

These counts describe the eligible opening-state pool inventory, not fixed report odds. The first report is drawn from a uniform shuffle of eligible team-era pools, but the next two must use different teams. Later rounds prefer teams you have not opened, and drafted players are removed before pools are rebuilt. Miami's deepest opening inventory is three shooting-guard pools and three sixth-man pools.

The 2000s guards: Wade or Payton?

The 2000s Heat point guard pool has five players: Dwyane Wade (95, as a secondary position), Gary Payton (92), Penny Hardaway (90), Tim Hardaway (88) and Terry Porter (87). Wade's higher rating and Payton's natural fit cancel out almost exactly: they are within about 0.01 team power before chemistry. Payton brings 96 defense, Wade brings 94 offense, so pick the one your lineup is missing. Both have the 88+ playmaking for the +5 Elite Playmaker bonus.

The same five players make up the 2000s shooting guard pool, where Wade plays his natural position and is the clear pick.

Where LeBron fits in Miami

For the Heat, LeBron appears only in the 2010s pools: small forward (his natural spot), power forward and sixth man. At small forward he is about 1.3 team power stronger than Jimmy Butler. At power forward, even at 95%, he leads Chris Bosh by about 1.2.

A 2010s Heat point guard pool would include LeBron, Wade and Tyler Herro, but three players is not enough for the game to show it.

Centers: only the 2010s pool

Shaquille O'Neal (97) and Alonzo Mourning (90) both played center in Miami, but the 2000s Heat center pool has only three players (Shaq, Mourning and Jermaine O'Neal) and the 1990s pool has one. You can draft Shaq and Mourning as 2000s power forwards or sixth men instead.

That leaves the 2010s center pool: Amar'e Stoudemire (90), Chris Bosh (90), Jermaine O'Neal (89) and Bam Adebayo (88). Only Adebayo is a natural center, yet Adebayo, Bosh and O'Neal are within 0.1 team power of each other before chemistry. Stoudemire trails by about 0.45 because of his 72 defense. For the +4 Lockdown Anchor bonus (a center with 88+ defense), take Adebayo (92) or Jermaine O'Neal (90); Bosh (82) and Stoudemire (72) do not qualify.
