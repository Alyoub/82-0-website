Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit 82-0.studio and play its free browser games, 82-0, 17-0, 162-0 and Echofall. The website is run by 82-0 Studio. The 17-0 and 162-0 privacy pages add details for those games.

In short: you do not need an account. The game code keeps progress in this browser and does not upload those game records. Visiting the site still sends network information to GitHub, Google Fonts and Google AdSense; AdSense may use cookies, local storage and other identifiers.

1. Information you give us

The browser games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and there are no player accounts. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond and handle the request. You can ask us to delete messages we still hold.

2. Game data saved in your browser

The games save your progress in your browser's local storage on your device:

  • the draft or season you are playing, so you can pick up where you left off;
  • your completed seasons, records and trophies;
  • your settings, such as sound, vibration and simulation speed.
  • in Echofall, scanned worlds, collected resources, upgrades, current location and expedition completion.

Our game code reads and writes this data only in your browser's localStorage; it contains no code that uploads those game records to 82-0 Studio. Clearing site data removes it. This statement is about saved game records, not the network and advertising data described below.

3. Services that load with the website

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages): GitHub receives requests needed to deliver the site. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Requests also normally include details such as browser type, requested URL and referrer. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): pages request Inter and Outfit from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Those requests reach Google with normal network data, including your IP address and browser information. See Google Fonts and privacy.
  • Analytics: the deployed source contains no separate analytics library or analytics account tag. Google AdSense still performs advertising delivery, fraud prevention and measurement; it is not covered by this no-analytics statement.
  • Cookies and browser storage: our first-party JavaScript does not call the cookie API. It does use localStorage for game progress, histories and settings. Google AdSense may use cookies, local storage and other identifiers as described in section 4.
  • Sharing results: when you share a result, the text is copied to your clipboard or passed to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The public pages and browser games load a Google AdSense tag to request and show ads. The current source loads that tag immediately; it does not pause the first Google ad request until a visitor makes a consent choice.

  • Before a consent choice: the AdSense request can send Google your IP address, browser/device details, requested page, referrer and existing Google identifiers. The site source does not itself determine whether Google treats that request as personalized, non-personalized or limited; that can depend on region, browser signals, prior choices and AdSense account configuration.
  • If personalized ads are allowed: Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or other identifiers and activity from this site and other sites to select personalized ads.
  • If personalized ads are not allowed: Google says non-personalized ads do not use past behavior for ad selection, but may still use cookies or identifiers for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. Limited ads still use IP addresses to deliver ads; programmatic limited ads can also use local storage and an invalid-traffic cookie.
  • Consent messages: no consent-management interface is implemented in this repository. Google can serve a Funding Choices consent message through AdSense account settings, which are outside this codebase, so this source audit cannot confirm which message is active for every visitor or region.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google's privacy policy.

5. Your choices

  • Delete game data: in any game, open Settings to abandon the current run or delete your season history. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything the games saved.
  • Ads: turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, or use the opt-out links in section 4.
  • Emails: ask us to delete any emails you sent us.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. We do not keep your game data, so requests to us are usually about emails you sent. For data used by Google's ads, use Google's own controls linked above.

6. Children

The site is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. 82-0 Studio does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly. Hosting, font and advertising providers still process requests as described above.

7. Separate historical notice: former 82-0 iPhone app

This section is not the website policy. The former 82-0: Basketball Draft app is no longer promoted on this website, but people may still have an older build installed. The app source and a distributable build are not present in this website repository, so this audit could not independently verify the data practices of the installed app.

Earlier versions of this policy described on-device game storage and optional integrations with Apple Game Center, Apple in-app purchases, RevenueCat, Google AdMob and nearby-device multiplayer. Treat that as a historical description, not a verified statement about every installed version or whether those external services still retain data. Check the app's permissions, Game Center and subscription settings on the device. Uninstalling removes the app's local container, but does not necessarily delete information held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, another device or an email provider.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
