Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026 | Last Updated: November 5, 2026

At 82-0: Basketball Draft ("we," "our," or "the App"), developed by Mouad Bousbaa, protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices when playing 82-0 on iOS and iPadOS devices and when you visit the 82-0.studio website.

Summary: 82-0 does not require an account to play. Your drafted lineups, season outcomes, and personal records are stored locally on your device unless you choose to use Apple Game Center.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or require your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise GPS location to play the core single-player draft and simulation game.

2. On-Device Local Data

The following information is saved locally on your device via standard iOS secure storage:

  • Current draft states, completed five-man rosters, and ratings.
  • Simulated 82-game season win-loss records and box score logs.
  • Dynasty save slots, personal historical records, and best runs.
  • Gameplay audio and haptics preferences.

3. Apple Game Center

If you enable Apple Game Center, the app may transmit your high scores calculated as (Wins × 100) + (Chemistry × 10) to the global Apple Game Center leaderboard. Apple manages your Game Center profile, username, and friend achievements according to the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

The app offers optional subscriptions for advanced player and team analytics (Hoop Dynasty Pro). Financial transactions are processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store StoreKit framework. We integrate RevenueCat to validate subscription status and manage entitlements anonymously. We never receive or store your credit card numbers or banking information.

5. Advertising & Consent Management (Google AdMob / UMP)

Free versions of the app may display advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). We utilize Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to comply with privacy frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency):

  • You are prompted for your advertising consent preferences where legally required.
  • You can adjust or revoke your ad choices at any time inside the app via Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Non-personalized ads are served if tracking consent is denied.

6. Website Advertising (Google AdSense)

The 82-0.studio website, including the free web versions of 82-0, 17-0 and 162-0, displays advertisements served by Google AdSense:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

7. Local Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer

When using local multiplayer, the app utilizes Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework over nearby Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Data transmitted is limited to draft spins and player picks between the two connected devices. No audio, video, or external network data is routed through third-party servers.

8. Children’s Privacy

82-0 is designed for general audiences and sports enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
